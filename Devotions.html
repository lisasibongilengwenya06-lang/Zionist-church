Verse of the Week

"The verse of the week is Isaiah 55:6
Seek the lord while you can find him. Call on him now while he is near.
Seek the lord while you can find him. Call on him now while he is near. Let the wicked change their ways and banish the thought of doing wrong. Let them turn to the Lord that he may have mercy on them. Yes, turn to our God, for he will forgive generously."
It is never too late for you as you are not too far from God. Seek him, Call on him and change your ways. He will be near to offer his mercy. He created, he loves you and he wants a relationship with you! So while you still can seek him and call on him for he is our loving heavenly father.
