Devotions

Weekly prayers, scriptures, and reflections for the Zionist community. Click any item below to read more.

It has been announced that at Rosebank College Braamfontein campus, a student named Ntloneloyamihlayonke Sopangisa who was in her final year studying a Diploma in Digital Marketing has sadly passed away. We keep her family and friends in our prayers and send our deepest condolences. We ask that we all keep her family and friends in our prayers during this difficult time.


"The Lord is close to the brokenhearted and saves those who are crushed in spirit" — Psalm 34:18

The verse of the week is Isaiah 55:6


"Seek the Lord while you can find him. Call on him now while he is near. Let the wicked change their ways and banish the thought of doing wrong. Let them turn to the Lord that he may have mercy on them. Yes, turn to our God, for he will forgive generously."

It is never too late for you as you are not too far from God. Seek him, call on him and change your ways. He will be near to offer his mercy. He created you, he loves you and he wants a relationship with you! So while you still can, seek him and call on him — for he is our loving Heavenly Father.

This week we reflect on the power of community within the Zionist church. From the mountains of Moria to the streets of Diepsloot, our churches have always been places of refuge, healing, and belonging.


Whether you are ZCC, Shembe, Zion, or Methodist — we are bound together by faith, by spirit, and by our shared African heritage. Let us continue to support one another through prayer, service, and love.


"For where two or three gather in my name, there am I with them." — Matthew 18:20
