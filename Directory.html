The different zionist church branches

& the locations

Limpopo - Zion Christian Church (ZCC)

Its headquarters is in the Limpopo province in the city of Moria. There are branches of ZCC in all provinces of South Africa but most are found in Gauteng. They are located in:

  • Moria
  • Alexander
  • Diepsloot
  • Cosmo city
  • George goch

KwaZulu-Natal - AmaNazaretha(Shembe Church)

It is the Nazareth baptist church that was founded in 1910/1911 by Isaiah Mloyiswa Mdliwamafa Shembe. It blends christianity with zulu traditions. The churches are in rural and suburban areas of KwaZulu-Natal. They are located in:

  • Ethekwini near Buffelsdraai and Verulam
  • eNanda
  • Doornfontein Gauteng, Johannesburg

Gauteng - AmaZion

There are different zion churches throughout the province, such as AmaZion weGalile, Gospel church in Zion and Jerusalem church of Zion. Some of these churches are of Zimbabwean decent but are based in South Africa. They are located in:

  • Diepsloot
  • Alexander
  • Honeydew

Methodist

There are different methodist churches throughout the country of South Africa and outside of South Africa such as Botswana and/or Zimbabwe. They are located in:

  • Gauteng, Soweto
  • Eastern cape South Africa
  • Botswana Gaborone/ Francistown
  • Zimbabwe, Bulawayo
