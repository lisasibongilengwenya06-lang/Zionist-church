About Us

History

Zionist churches were established in 1904 by missionaries from Chicago, the African members then incorporated African spirituality which was defined as the second baptism of the spirit. Zionist churches are mostly found in southern Africa, they have a rich rooted history in the Christian catholic apostolic church, the churches would incorporate the words Apostolic, Pentecostal or Faith to represent their biblical charter for example Gospel church in Zion. The Zion churches have different branches of churches such as the Zion Christian church known as the ZCC of Limpopo, AmaNazaretha formally known as the Shembe church or iBandla lamaNazaretha and AmaSiyoni or AmaKrestu of KwaZulu Natal, methiodist and so much more. The zion churches have these 2 key characteristics being:

  • Faith healing and prophecy – Zionist believe in the power of healing through the holy spirit and prophecy.It is deeply intertwined in spiritual practices, prophecy is seen as divine intervation carrying spiritual healing.

  • Community and identity – Zionist churches believe in providing a sense of community for black south Africans that help preserve African spirituality while incorporating Christianity.It is interconnected with community, shaping spiritual identies through shared values and social interactions.

Mission and Vision

The vision and mission of the Zionist churches is to create a spirit filled community of believers rooted in African spirituality and Christianity. Claiming the kingdom of God through worship and prayer with dignity and giving the identity to the African people by nurturing communities through the healing, worship, prophecy, prayer and culture.
